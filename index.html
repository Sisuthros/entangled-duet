How it works

01Quantum randomness picks the setting. True QRNG bits from Hadamard superposition choose tempo, mode, and arpeggio density — not a pseudo-random seed.
02Entangled states pick the form. Bell pairs |Φ⁺⟩, |Ψ⁺⟩ and GHZ states are prepared and measured on the chip. The measured correlation E decides whether the voices sing in unison, drift free, or mirror each other.
03Aurora composes what physics cannot. The theme, harmony, and orchestration are hers — the quantum computer decides the relationships. Human-scale craft, physics-scale truth.
04Every note is auditable. Job IDs are verifiable on OriginQ Cloud. Download the provenance JSON and check the collapse counts yourself.
